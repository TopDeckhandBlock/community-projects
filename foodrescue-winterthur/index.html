Winterthur · Switzerland

Good bread should not end up in the bin.

Bakeries and small grocers in Winterthur have perfectly good bread and vegetables left at closing time. Our volunteer riders collect it by bike and bring it to two social kitchens and a public sharing shelf. Around 300 kg per week is rescued.

Why we exist

A bakery that closes at 18:30 cannot sell tomorrow what is left on the shelf. Throwing it away costs money and is a waste of everything that went into producing it. We collect within an hour of closing, on cargo bikes and trailers, and deliver while the food is still good. Everything is documented, so shops can see where their surplus goes.

What we do

Evening pickups

Monday to Saturday between 18:00 and 20:00, routes of 20 to 40 minutes by bike.

Sharing shelf

A public shelf at the Neumarkt where anyone can take bread and vegetables, no questions asked.

Social kitchens

Two kitchens cook with our deliveries: one for a women's shelter, one for a neighbourhood lunch.

How it works

1

Shops announce surplus

A short message at closing time: what is left and how much. No paperwork.

2

A rider takes the route

Riders see the open pickups and claim one. Each route has a backup rider.

3

Delivery confirmed

The kitchen or shelf confirms the drop-off, the kilograms go into the monthly counter.
