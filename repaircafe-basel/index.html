Bring it broken. We fix it together.

We run repair cafés in three neighbourhoods of Basel. Volunteer repairers fix electronics, bicycles, lamps and small appliances instead of letting people throw them away. Around 60 items get a second life every month.

Why we exist

A broken toaster is not waste, it is usually a five-euro part and twenty minutes of somebody who knows how it works. Repair cafés keep appliances out of the incinerator, save their owners money and pass practical knowledge from one generation to the next. Everything happens in the open: the owner sits next to the repairer and sees what is being done.

What we do

Monthly repair café

Kleinbasel, Gundeldingen and Riehen on rotating Saturdays, 10:00 to 16:00. Bring one item, stay while it is being repaired.

Bike workshop

Every second Wednesday: brakes, gears, punctures. Tools and stands are there, you do the work with guidance.

Repair guides

Our repairers write down what they fixed and how, so the knowledge does not leave with them.

How it works

1

Tell us what is broken

Send a short description and a photo. We tell you whether we can help and which date fits.

2

Reserve a slot

Slots are 45 minutes. Small appliances and bikes are the most common cases.

3

Fix it together

You sit next to the repairer. If a spare part is needed, we tell you where to get it cheaply.
