Solar for people who rent.

Renters in Groningen want a small solar panel on the balcony but get lost in rules, subsidies and technical choices. We run free advice evenings, keep a buying guide and organise group orders so members pay less.

Why we exist

Around half of Groningen households rent, and a renter cannot put panels on the roof. A plug-in balcony panel is the realistic alternative: it feeds the base load of the flat, it moves with you when you move, and it needs no permission from the grid operator in most cases. The hard part is not the hardware, it is knowing which rules apply to your building.

What we do

Advice evenings

Every six weeks, free, in the community centre. Bring your energy bill and a photo of your balcony.

Buying guide

An honest guide to panel types, inverter limits and what the Dutch rules actually require, updated twice a year.

Group orders

We collect orders per batch and negotiate a price. Members installed 180 systems this way so far.

How it works

1

Check your situation

Rented or owned, south or west facing, balcony railing or wall mount. The guide walks you through it.

2

Join an advice evening

One hour, no sales pitch, no companies in the room. You leave knowing what to buy and what to avoid.

3

Order with the group

We place the batch order, you pay the supplier directly. Delivery and installation instructions included.
