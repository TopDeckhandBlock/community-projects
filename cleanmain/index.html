Why we exist

Rivers are where a city meets its own waste. The Main bank between Osthafen and the Eiserner Steg is walked by families every weekend, and after every flood the grass is full of bottles, bags and styrofoam. We go out with gloves, grabbers and a weighing scale, sort what can be recycled, and publish the numbers so the problem stays visible.

What we do

Monthly cleanups

First Saturday of every month, 9:00 to 12:00. Gloves, bags and grabbers are provided. Families welcome, children from age 6 with an adult.

Spot reports

Anyone can send us a photo and a location of a badly littered spot. We map it, verify it and put it on the next cleanup route.

School workshops

Two-hour sessions for classes 5 to 8 about plastic in rivers, sorting and what actually happens to waste in Frankfurt.

How it works
